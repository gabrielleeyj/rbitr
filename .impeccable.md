# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Two equally important audiences: platform/DevOps engineers who deploy the gateway, register tools, and author policies; and enterprise compliance/security teams who review approval requests, risk overrides, and evidence exports. Both work in desktop-first, session-based contexts (configuring, reviewing, exporting) but the UI must remain usable on smaller screens for on-call approval reviews.

### Brand Personality
Bold and distinctive. rbitr should not look like a generic enterprise dashboard. Confidence and authority (this is a governance control plane — it must feel like it is in control) expressed through a strong visual identity: decisive use of the purple brand color (#7734be), strong typographic hierarchy, and intentional composition. Bold does not mean loud everywhere — precision and legibility still win in dense tables and evidence views.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Brand color: purple #7734be as the anchor; neutrals should tint toward it rather than staying pure gray.
- Light and dark themes are both first-class and must both meet contrast requirements.
- Anti-references: generic AI-dashboard aesthetic (cyan-on-dark, gradient text, glassmorphism, identical card grids), default shadcn look with no customization.
- Distinctiveness should come from typography, color discipline, and layout rhythm — not decorative effects.

### Accessibility
WCAG 2.1 AA is the bar (enterprise/marketplace procurement requirement). 4.5:1 text contrast, keyboard operability, visible focus, proper labels/roles.

### Design Principles
1. Authority through restraint — bold identity, calm execution; dense data stays legible.
2. Purple is the signature — use it decisively for primary actions and identity, never dilute it across every surface.
3. Both themes are real — every color decision must hold up in light and dark.
4. Evidence-grade clarity — compliance users must trust what they see; hierarchy and labels over decoration.
5. Not-another-shadcn-dashboard — customize tokens, type, and layout so the product is recognizable.
